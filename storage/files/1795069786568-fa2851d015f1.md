# Lapu-Lapu City College — Board of Trustees
## Resolution No. 2026-04: Institutional Records Governance Charter

Campus: Gun-ob, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu
Effective Date: February 1, 2026
Sponsoring Office: Office of the College President

### Section 1. Purpose
This charter mandates that all official memoranda, BOT resolutions, CHED curricula, UniFAST rosters, financial reports, and contracts generated by Lapu-Lapu City College offices be indexed in the central LLCC Document Repository.

### Section 2. Classification & Access
1. Internal: Accessible to all authenticated LLCC faculty and administrative personnel.
2. Restricted: Limited to Deans, Department Heads, and Records Officers.
3. Confidential: Accessible only to the Office of the College President and designated auditors.

### Section 3. Version Control
Every substantive amendment requires a minor (x.1) or major (x.0) version increment accompanied by verifiable change notes.
