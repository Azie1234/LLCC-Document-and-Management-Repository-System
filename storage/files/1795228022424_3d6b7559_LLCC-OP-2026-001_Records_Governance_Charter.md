# Lapu-Lapu City College — Board of Trustees
## Resolution No. 2026-04: Institutional Records Governance Charter

Campus: Gun-ob, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu
Effective Date: February 1, 2026

### Section 1. Purpose
All official memoranda, BOT resolutions, CHED curricula, UniFAST rosters, financial reports, and official letters generated by Lapu-Lapu City College offices must be indexed in the LLCC Document Management Repository.

### Section 2. Classification
1. Internal: Accessible to authenticated personnel and authorized students.
2. Restricted: Limited to Department Heads, Faculty/Staff, and Administrators.
3. Confidential: Executive and designated compliance auditors only.
