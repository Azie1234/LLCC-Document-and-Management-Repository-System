# Lapu-Lapu City College — Office of Student Affairs
## UniFAST RA 10931 Free Higher Education Billing Guidelines

### Eligibility Checklist
1. Bonafide Filipino citizen admitted to a CHED-recognized LLCC undergraduate degree program.
2. Validated Certificate of Registration (COR) issued by the LLCC Registrar.
3. No prior undergraduate degree completed and within maximum residency rule.

### Billing Timeline
Registrar & OSA shall reconcile enrollment rosters within 15 working days after the last day of late registration.
